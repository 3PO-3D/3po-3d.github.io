        <div class="pillar-body" hidden><div class="pillar-body-inner">
          <p>A semi-automated system for generating patient-specific, 3D-printed orthopedic walking casts from 3D body scans. Breathable. Washable. Adjustable. Printable in a living room. Designed to replace a 150-year-old technology — the plaster cast — with something that doesn't turn a recovery into a punishment.</p>
          <p class="cast-product-label">The walking cast</p>
          <p>The cast snaps on with magnets and two wide velcro straps — one around the calf, one threaded through the outsole. As your calf shrinks during recovery, the straps adjust. The outsole has a structural void in the centre that collapses when you stand, giving a flatter contact surface, but rolls forward on each step so you can walk without needing ankle flexion. Held by magnets and physics.</p>
          <p class="cast-product-label">The insole</p>
          <p>Every walking cast needs an orthopedic insole. So I built one. Infill pattern and density are tuned per person — foot shape, arch type, body weight — soft where comfort matters and firm where support does. Thin enough to fit inside most shoes. It comes with the cast. It's not an afterthought.</p>
          <p>The method exists. The code exists. The printers exist. What doesn't exist yet is the funding and the regulatory infrastructure to manufacture it at scale. This one is a long game, and I'm still playing it.</p>

(function(){
  var rows=document.querySelectorAll('.pillar-row');
  rows.forEach(function(row){
    var btn=row.querySelector('.pillar-header'), body=row.querySelector('.pillar-body'), icon=row.querySelector('.pillar-header__toggle');
    if(!btn||!body) return;
    btn.addEventListener('click', function(){
      var open=btn.getAttribute('aria-expanded')==='true';
      if(open){ btn.setAttribute('aria-expanded','false'); body.hidden=true; icon.textContent='+'; row.classList.remove('is-open'); }
      else {
        rows.forEach(function(o){ if(o!==row){ var b=o.querySelector('.pillar-header'),bb=o.querySelector('.pillar-body'),i=o.querySelector('.pillar-header__toggle'); if(b)b.setAttribute('aria-expanded','false'); if(bb)bb.hidden=true; if(i)i.textContent='+'; o.classList.remove('is-open'); } });
        btn.setAttribute('aria-expanded','true'); body.hidden=false; icon.textContent='−'; row.classList.add('is-open');
      }
    });
  });
})();
