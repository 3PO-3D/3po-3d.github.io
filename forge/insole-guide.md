  <p class="mono-label">Insole scan guide</p>
  <h1>How to scan your foot</h1>
  <p class="lead">Your insoles are shaped from a 3D scan of your foot. This guide walks you through the scan using <strong>Kiri Engine</strong> — a free app on your phone. It takes about 10 minutes.</p>

  <!-- What you need -->
  <div class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step__n" aria-hidden="true">✓</div>
    <div class="guide-step__body">
      <h3>What you need</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Your smartphone (iPhone or Android)</li>
        <li>The <strong>Kiri Engine</strong> app — free download below</li>
        <li>A sheet of <strong>A4 paper</strong></li>
        <li>A plain floor and decent lighting — no direct sunlight</li>
      </ul>
      <div class="guide-apps">
        <a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/kiri-engine/id1604883895" target="_blank" rel="noopener">App Store →</a>
        <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kiri.engine" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Play →</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <hr class="guide-divider">

  <!-- Step 1 -->
  <div class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step__n">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step__body">
      <h3>Set up</h3>
      <p>Place a sheet of <strong>A4 paper flat on the floor</strong> and stand your bare foot on it. Keep your foot still and relaxed — flat on the paper, as natural as possible.</p>
      <div class="guide-step__img">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/1..jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Foot placed on A4 paper on the floor">
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Step 2 -->
  <div class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step__n">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step__body">
      <h3>Open Kiri Engine and start a scan</h3>
      <ol style="color:var(--muted);line-height:1.7;margin:0 0 0.75rem 1.1rem;">
        <li>Open the app → tap <strong>+</strong> → <strong>Object Scan</strong></li>
        <li>Choose <strong>High Detail</strong> mode</li>
        <li>Point the camera at your foot from about <strong>50 cm away</strong></li>
      </ol>
      <div class="guide-step__imgs">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/2.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Kiri Engine new scan screen">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/3.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Selecting High Detail mode">
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Step 3 -->
  <div class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step__n">3</div>
    <div class="guide-step__body">
      <h3>Scan your foot</h3>
      <p>The most important data is the <strong>arch, heel shape, and sole profile</strong> — so get the camera as low as possible. The best technique: <strong>place your phone on the floor</strong> and slowly circle around your foot, keeping the camera aimed at the foot at ground level. This gives the clearest view of the arch.</p>
      <p>Aim for <strong>60–80 photos</strong>, or use <strong>video mode</strong> (video captures a continuous stream of frames and is often more accurate — both free). If you shoot video, send the video file along with the scan link.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Move slowly and steadily — blurry frames break the mesh</li>
        <li>Keep your foot <strong>completely still</strong> during the scan</li>
        <li>Avoid deep shadows; a lamp on the opposite side helps</li>
      </ul>
      <div class="guide-step__imgs">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/4.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Camera positioned low, circling the foot">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/5.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Scanning around the heel and arch">
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Step 4 -->
  <div class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step__n">4</div>
    <div class="guide-step__body">
      <h3>Process the scan</h3>
      <p>Tap <strong>Done</strong> → <strong>Process</strong> and wait for the 3D model to generate (1–3 minutes in the app, a bit longer on cloud processing).</p>
      <div class="guide-step__imgs">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/6.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Tapping Done after scanning">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/7.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Processing screen in Kiri Engine">
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Step 5 -->
  <div class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step__n">5</div>
    <div class="guide-step__body">
      <h3>Send me the scan</h3>
      <p>Once processing is complete, tap <strong>Share / Export</strong>. Kiri Engine generates a download link (looks like <code>kiriengine.app/share/d…</code>). Copy it.</p>
      <p>Send the scan in <strong>one of these two ways only</strong> — any other method won't be connected to your order:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Reply to your order email</strong> — paste the <code>kiriengine.app/share/…</code> download link in the reply</li>
        <li><strong>Upload directly to Dropbox</strong> — use the private upload link in your order email to drop the file into your order folder</li>
      </ul>
      <div class="guide-note">
        <strong>Also include your EU shoe size</strong> in the same message if you haven't already. If you forget, I'll follow up — but including it speeds things up.
      </div>
      <div class="guide-step__img" style="margin-top:1rem;">
        <img src="{{ '/assets/img/guides/kiri_instructions/8.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" alt="Kiri Engine share / export screen">
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <hr class="guide-divider">

  <p style="color:var(--muted);font-size:0.9rem;line-height:1.7;"><strong style="color:var(--text);">That's it.</strong> I'll confirm receipt within 1 business day and let you know when your insoles are in the print queue.</p>
