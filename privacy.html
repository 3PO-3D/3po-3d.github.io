3. Who we share data with (processors)

We use the following providers, who process data solely on our instructions:

  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland) — payment processing.
  • Számlázz.hu (KBOSS.hu Kft.) — invoicing and the mandatory NAV Online Invoice data reporting.
  • Make — transferring order data between our systems.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd.) — sending the confirmation emails (Gmail) and the website's fonts (Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it loads).
  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Dropbox — storage of order files.

Some providers operate outside the EU (e.g. the USA); such transfers are based on the providers' standard contractual clauses (SCCs) or an adequacy decision. We do not sell your data and do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Your rights

You may request access to your data, its rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise these rights, write to 3po@3po3d.com. We respond within one month.

5. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH): 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11.; ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu; naih.hu. You may also turn to a court.

6. Data security

We use reputable providers and an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we limit access to order data to those who need it for fulfilment.

7. Children

The service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under the age of 16.

8. Cookies

The website uses only strictly necessary cookies — placed by the website and by Stripe to carry out and secure the payment. These do not require consent and cannot be switched off, because the website would not work without them. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but then payment will not work.
